🎥 Real-Time Image Classifier

Powered by CIFAR-10 CNN Model

📊 How It Works

This app uses a Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) trained on CIFAR-10 dataset to classify objects in real-time from your webcam.

🎯 Accuracy

The model achieves ~70% accuracy on test data. For best results, show clear, well-lit objects similar to CIFAR-10 images.

⚡ Performance

Predictions are made in real-time at ~10-30 FPS depending on your hardware and network conditions.
